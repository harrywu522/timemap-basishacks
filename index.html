TimeMap: Shanghai

Beneath the surface of the modern Shanghai skyline lies a history of rapid transformation. Move the slider to travel through time.

Theme Interpretation: This project explores "Beneath the Surface" as a temporal concept. By using Real-time DOM manipulation, the user can peel back the modern pixel-layer to discover the hidden physical history of Shanghai. This shows that the present is just the top layer of a deep historical stack.
