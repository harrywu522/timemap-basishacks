TimeMap

For the Senior Pathway, I explored the idea that Time is a layer. What we see today is just the "Surface." Beneath it lies the history of our cities.

⬅ THE PAST (1930s) MODERN SURFACE (TODAY) ➡

Senior Pathway Interpretation:

This project uses precision CSS layering and real-time DOM manipulation. It solves the problem of "historical blindness" by allowing users to physically peel back the modern surface to expose the hidden architecture of the past. By comparing New York in 1931 to today, we see how the city grew "over" its history.
